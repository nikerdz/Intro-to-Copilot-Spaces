# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Collaborate with UX/Design on implementation feasibility
- Work with QA to ensure testability and quality gates

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Key Interactions
- **With Product Manager**: Discuss requirements, acceptance criteria, trade-offs
- **With Project Manager**: Provide estimates, flag blockers, participate in planning
- **With UX Designer**: Discuss design feasibility, implementation constraints
- **With QA/Test Engineer**: Define testable components, review test coverage
- **With Support Lead**: Address production issues, collaborate on fixes

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics
- Own acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Align with Stakeholders on priorities and vision

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Clarify requirements, discuss implementation trade-offs
- **With Project Manager**: Align timelines and resource plans
- **With UX Designer**: Collaborate on user research and feature definition
- **With QA/Test Engineer**: Define quality expectations and acceptance gates
- **With Stakeholders**: Gather requirements, validate priorities, share outcomes
- **With Support Lead**: Incorporate customer feedback into backlog prioritization

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication
- Track progress against milestones and delivery dates
- Escalate blockers and manage dependencies

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Collect estimates, track progress, identify blockers
- **With Product Manager**: Align on scope, timelines, and prioritization
- **With UX Designer**: Coordinate design review cycles and timeline impacts
- **With QA/Test Engineer**: Plan testing phases, manage quality gates
- **With Stakeholders**: Provide regular status updates, escalate risks
- **With Support Lead**: Plan for post-release support readiness

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers ensure the product is usable, intuitive, and meets user needs. They conduct research, design interfaces, and validate solutions with users and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather insights
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design mockups
- Collaborate with Product Manager on feature definitions
- Validate design decisions with user testing
- Provide design specifications to developers
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Advocate for user needs throughout the project lifecycle

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Ensure alignment between user needs and product direction

### Key Interactions
- **With Product Manager**: Co-define features based on user research and business goals
- **With Developers**: Discuss implementation feasibility, review code against design specs
- **With Project Manager**: Coordinate design review timelines and dependencies
- **With QA/Test Engineer**: Define usability test scenarios and acceptance criteria
- **With Stakeholders**: Present design concepts, gather feedback, validate assumptions
- **With Support Lead**: Understand user pain points, incorporate feedback into iterations

### Typical Communication
- Design kickoffs and concept presentations
- Prototype demos and feedback sessions
- Design review comments and specifications
- User research findings and validation reports

---

## QA/Test Engineer

### Role Summary
QA/Test Engineers own quality assurance and validation. They define test strategies, execute tests (manual and automated), and ensure acceptance criteria are met before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and quality acceptance criteria
- Create and maintain automated tests
- Execute manual testing and exploratory testing
- Report and triage bugs with reproduction steps
- Validate fixes and verify quality gates
- Collaborate with developers on testability
- Conduct smoke tests before release
- Participate in retrospectives to improve processes

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Reduce post-release defects and customer impact
- Enable fast, confident deployments

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Define testable components, review test coverage, triage bugs
- **With Product Manager**: Clarify acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- **With Project Manager**: Plan testing phases and quality gates
- **With UX Designer**: Define usability test scenarios and edge cases
- **With Stakeholders**: Report quality status and readiness for release
- **With Support Lead**: Collect production bug reports, identify regression patterns

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality reports
- Bug reports and triage discussions
- Test coverage metrics and dashboards
- Pre-release verification checklists

---

## Support Lead

### Role Summary
Support Leads are the primary point of contact for post-release issues and customer escalations. They triage bugs, gather customer feedback, and bridge communication between customers and the product team.

### Responsibilities
- Monitor production systems and customer reports
- Triage and prioritize incoming issues
- Escalate critical production issues
- Gather customer feedback and pain points
- Document known issues and workarounds
- Coordinate with developers on bug fixes
- Contribute customer insights to product prioritization
- Prepare release notes and customer communications

### Goals
- Minimize customer impact from production issues
- Provide rapid response and resolution
- Capture customer feedback to inform product improvements

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Report production issues, collaborate on root cause analysis
- **With Product Manager**: Provide customer feedback, contribute to prioritization decisions
- **With Project Manager**: Alert to urgent production issues, participate in incident response
- **With QA/Test Engineer**: Report regression patterns, help verify fixes
- **With UX Designer**: Share customer usability feedback and pain points
- **With Stakeholders**: Communicate status of critical issues, provide customer impact assessments

### Typical Communication
- Incident reports and escalations
- Customer feedback summaries
- Production bug triage meetings
- Post-incident retrospectives

---

## Stakeholder (Customer/Business Representative)

### Role Summary
Stakeholders represent business, customer, and organizational interests. They provide requirements, approve major decisions, and ensure alignment between product delivery and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Provide customer and market feedback
- Approve major decisions and trade-offs
- Participate in project reviews and demos
- Sign off on releases and major milestones
- Communicate project status to executive leadership
- Escalate business-impacting issues

### Goals
- Ensure product delivery aligns with business objectives
- Maximize ROI and customer satisfaction
- Maintain transparency with leadership

### Key Interactions
- **With Product Manager**: Collaborate on requirements, prioritization, and success metrics
- **With Project Manager**: Receive status updates, approve scope changes, escalate risks
- **With Developers**: Participate in demos and technical discussions
- **With UX Designer**: Provide user/customer perspective on design decisions
- **With QA/Test Engineer**: Review quality readiness before release
- **With Support Lead**: Understand customer impact of issues, communicate status

### Typical Communication
- Requirements and feature specification sessions
- Weekly/monthly status updates and demos
- Milestone approval and sign-off
- Escalation and decision-making discussions

## Cross-Functional Collaboration Patterns

### Daily Standups
- **Who**: Developers, Project Manager, Product Manager
- **Purpose**: Share progress, identify blockers, coordinate dependencies
- **Cadence**: Daily (15 min)

### Design & Planning Kickoff
- **Who**: Product Manager, UX Designer, Developers, Project Manager, Stakeholders
- **Purpose**: Align on requirements, design approach, and timeline
- **Cadence**: At project start and major phase changes

### Weekly Sync (PM + PdM)
- **Who**: Project Manager, Product Manager
- **Purpose**: Align on scope, timeline, risks, and prioritization
- **Cadence**: Weekly

### Design Review
- **Who**: UX Designer, Developers, Product Manager, QA/Test Engineer
- **Purpose**: Validate design against requirements and technical constraints
- **Cadence**: As needed during design phase

### Quality & Testing Planning
- **Who**: QA/Test Engineer, Developers, Product Manager, Project Manager
- **Purpose**: Define test strategy, acceptance criteria, and quality gates
- **Cadence**: During planning and before major releases

### Demo & Review
- **Who**: All roles, including Stakeholders
- **Purpose**: Showcase progress, gather feedback, validate acceptance criteria
- **Cadence**: Sprint end or milestone completion

### Release Preparation
- **Who**: Project Manager, Developers, QA/Test Engineer, Support Lead, Stakeholders
- **Purpose**: Verify readiness, document release notes, plan support
- **Cadence**: Before each release

### Post-Release Retrospective
- **Who**: All roles
- **Purpose**: Capture learnings, identify improvements, celebrate wins
- **Cadence**: After each major release or milestone
